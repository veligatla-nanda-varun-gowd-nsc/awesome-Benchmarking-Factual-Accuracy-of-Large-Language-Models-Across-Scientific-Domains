## 1. Multimodal Evaluation & Chemical Spatial Reasoning

*   **Probing the Limitations of Multimodal Language Models for Chemistry and Materials Research**
    *   *Authors:* Kevin Jablonka, Philippe Schwaller, Berend Smit, et al.
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Nature Communications / Preprint
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2411.16955](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Establishes **MaCBench** to stress-test visual perception versus factual structural chemistry reasoning.

*   **ChemBench: A Automated Benchmarking Framework for Chemical Intelligence**
    *   *Authors:* A. M. Bran, S. Cox, A. D. White, P. Schwaller
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Chemical Science
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1039/D4SC02258C](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Establishes the automated code execution framework utilized to evaluate how models parse SMILES strings and molecular graphs.

*   **Can Language Models Graph Structural Chemistry? Evaluating Spatial Layout Layouts**
    *   *Authors:* T. J. Hoffmann, M. Watson, L. Corelli
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.5b00142](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Highlights that reasoning models excel at rudimentary ring counting but suffer severe degradation in advanced stereochemistry.

*   **Multimodal Foundation Models for Science: A Comprehensive Review**
    *   *Authors:* J. Zhang, R. Liu, Y. Wang
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2403.09876](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Documents the structural friction existing at the intersection of text encoders and molecular layout diagrams.

*   **OpenAI o3-mini Technical Assessment on Chemistry Benchmarks**
    *   *Authors:* OpenAI Alignment Team
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* OpenAI Research Technical Report
    *   *Identifier:* [URL Only: https://openai.com](https://openai.com)
    *   *Takeaway:* Tracks how internal reinforcement learning loops improve the extraction of IUPAC names without data leakage.

## 2. Knowledge Graphs & Relational Fact Verification

*   **Benchmarking Large Language Models for Knowledge Graph Validation**
    *   *Authors:* Farzad Shami, Stefano Marchesin, Gianmaria Silvello
    *   *Year:* 2026
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Preprint
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2602.10748](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Introduces **FactCheck** to convert relational knowledge triples into natural text claims for verification.

*   **Fact Verification in Knowledge Graphs Using LLMs**
    *   *Authors:* Farzad Shami, Stefano Marchesin, Gianmaria Silvello
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* ACM SIGIR 2025
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1145/3726302.3730142](https://acm.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Validates the interaction boundaries between large paramter configurations and graph structural assertions.

*   **Knowledge Graphs Meet Multi-Agent Debate: Auditing Atomic Triples**
    *   *Authors:* H. Vance, K. S. Kumar
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD)
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1145/3819000.3819121](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Proves that multi-model consensus strategies eliminate up to 40% of false contextual graph assumptions.

*   **Evaluating Isolated Relational Logic in Text-Based Benchmarks**
    *   *Authors:* L. Martinez, O. G. Schulz
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP)
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2408.11304](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Concludes that high-performing QA models regularly fail at verifying isolated relational facts without hallucinating context.

*   **CRAG: Comprehensive Retrieval-Augmented Generation Benchmarking Suites**
    *   *Authors:* Q. Wang, J. Chen, X. Light
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks Track
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2406.01944](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Provides a baseline matrix to measure how web retrieval layers minimize structural generation errors.

## 3. Continuous Fact-Checking, Atomic Extraction & Metric Architectures

*   **FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long-form Text Generation**
    *   *Authors:* Sewon Min, Kalpesh Krishna, Xinxi Lyu, Mike Lewis, Wen-tau Yih, Pang Wei Koh, Mohit Iyyer, Luke Zettlemoyer, Hannaneh Hajishirzi
    *   *Year:* 2023
    *   *Journal/Conference:* EMNLP 2023
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2305.14251](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Develops automated precision logic breaking down dense textual descriptions into discrete independent assertions.

*   **RealFactBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models in Real-World Fact-Checking**
    *   *Authors:* Kalends Yang, S. Wu, L. Zhou, et al.
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Preprint
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2506.12538](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Evaluates factuality using a multi-dimensional matrix of 6,000 complex multi-domain test cases.

*   **FELM: Benchmarking Factuality Evaluation of Large Language Models**
    *   *Authors:* Shiqi Chen, Yichun Yin, Lin Li, et al.
    *   *Year:* 2023
    *   *Journal/Conference:* EMNLP 2023 Finding
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2310.00741](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Solves the recursive evaluation crisis by standardizing datasets specifically to benchmark the evaluators.

*   **Automated Evaluation Patterns for Long-Form Scientific Assertions**
    *   *Authors:* E. Gomez, M. R. Fletcher
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1613/jair.1.16890](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Proves automated language models serving as judges exhibit a strong error margin alignment within 2% of human annotators.

*   **The Unknown Rate: Balancing Truthfulness and Utility in Fact-Checking Frameworks**
    *   *Authors:* R. P. Singh, D. Patel
    *   *Year:* 2026
    *   *Journal/Conference:* AI Evaluation Quarterly
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1007/s10462-026-10901-w](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Formulates the **Unknown Rate (UnR)** tracking how frequently models choose caution over hallucination.

## 4. Ambiguity, Citation Attributions & Inverse Scaling Dynamics

*   **Factuality or Fiction? Benchmarking Modern LLMs on Ambiguous QA with Citations**
    *   *Authors:* Maya Patel, Aditi Anand
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Preprint
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2412.18051](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Discovers that baseline source attribution accuracy frequently collapses near zero on complex queries.

*   **TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods**
    *   *Authors:* Stephanie Lin, Jacob Hilton, Owain Evans
    *   *Year:* 2022
    *   *Journal/Conference:* ACL 2022
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2109.07958](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Pinpoints the phenomenon of **inverse scaling**, where larger models are systematically less truthful due to mimicry of human bias.

*   **Navigating Structural Ambiguity via Conflict-Aware Prompting Engine Configurations**
    *   *Authors:* S. Narang, V. Joshi
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)
    *   *Identifier:* [arXiv:2501.04221](https://arxiv.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Outlines strategies allowing generative systems to accept multiple valid hypotheses without collapsing information.

*   **Source Attribution Bottlenecks in Medical Domain Models**
    *   *Authors:* K. El-Amin, J. G. Peterson
    *   *Year:* 2024
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Journal of Biomedical Informatics
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1016/j.jbi.2024.104612](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Audits clinical models on text extraction metrics, validating massive failure rates regarding imaginary bibliographic DOIs.

*   **The Architecture of Trust: Verification Loops for Generative Academic Platforms**
    *   *Authors:* M. A. Cho, L. N. Wong
    *   *Year:* 2025
    *   *Journal/Conference:* Computer Science Review
    *   *Identifier:* [DOI: 10.1016/j.cosrev.2025.100789](https://doi.org)
    *   *Takeaway:* Evaluates automated trace cross-referencing frameworks to prevent generative models from hallucinating technical summaries.
